Flourless Chocolate Cake

Three-layer cake with chocolate cream filling & agar agar mirror glaze

Equipment

  • Piping bags
  • Food thermometer
  • Cake spatula
  • Parchment paper
  • Hand mixer
  • Spatula & whisk
  • Mixing bowls
  • Measuring cup (for pouring glaze)
  • Fine mesh sieve

Note: Work quickly when straining the glaze — agar agar begins setting as it cools, and a very fine mesh can make this step difficult.

Flourless Cake

Whip the yolks with vanilla, incorporate the chocolate, then fold into stiffly whipped whites. Don't overmix.

Ingredients

  • 120g dark chocolate
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 4 eggs, separated — whites in a large bowl, yolks in a medium bowl
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 150g sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Melt chocolate and butter together over a double boiler (water at 160–175°F). Once smooth, stir in milk. Set aside to cool slightly.
  2. Whip the egg yolks with vanilla until pale and thickened. Fold into the chocolate mixture and set aside.
  3. Whip the egg whites with a pinch of salt to soft peaks. Gradually add sugar over 2–3 minutes and continue whipping to stiff, glossy peaks (8–12 minutes at high speed). Do this step last for a lighter, spongier cake.
  4. Fold ⅓ of the whites into the chocolate mixture to loosen it. Then pour the chocolate mixture into the remaining whites and fold gently until just combined — stop as soon as no white streaks remain.
  5. Line a 15×10 in pan with parchment and spread batter evenly. Bake at 340°F for about 20 minutes. Cool completely in the pan, then wrap tightly and refrigerate overnight.

Cocoa Syrup

A simple syrup brushed onto each layer before filling — creates a moisture barrier and adds flavor.

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup hot water
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients until fully dissolved. Set aside to cool completely before use.

Chocolate Cream Filling

Ingredients

  • 150g dark chocolate
  • ⅓ cup heavy cream
  • 1¼ cups cold heavy cream
  • 2½ tbsp sugar

Steps

  1. Melt chocolate with ⅓ cup heavy cream over a double boiler until smooth.
  2. Separately whip 1¼ cups cold heavy cream with sugar to medium peaks.
  3. Fold ⅓ of the whipped cream into the chocolate to loosen it, then fold in the rest.
  4. Flip the cold cake out of the pan and trim the edges. Cut into 3 equal pieces.
  5. Brush each layer with cocoa syrup. Pipe and spread the chocolate cream between each layer. Wrap and refrigerate overnight.

Chocolate Mirror Glaze

Mix dry and wet ingredients, bring to a boil, strain, and cool to 110°F before pouring in one confident, even pass.

Ingredients

  • 50g cocoa powder
  • 115g sugar
  • ½ tsp agar agar powder
  • ⅓ cup + 1 tbsp water
  • ⅓ cup + 1 tbsp heavy cream

Steps

  1. Mix dry ingredients in a saucepan until blended, then add liquids and stir to combine. Whisk until smooth with no lumps.
  2. Heat over medium, stirring constantly. Bring to a full boil (212°F) and hold for 90 seconds — do not exceed 220°F.
  3. Remove from heat and strain immediately through a fine mesh sieve into a bowl or measuring cup.
  4. Let cool, stirring gently every few minutes, until it reaches 110°F. Have the cold cake on a rack over a tray ready before you start.
  5. Pour the glaze in one smooth, confident pass from one end to the other — let gravity do the work. Do not go back over areas already glazed.
  6. Refrigerate 30 minutes to set, then slice into 7 pieces. Decorate with edible gold if desired.

Notes

  • Order of operations: Melt chocolate → whip yolks → combine chocolate and yolks → whip egg whites last → fold immediately. Never let the whites sit waiting.
  • Eggs: Separate while cold (easier), then let them come to room temperature about 30 minutes before starting.
  • Glaze: The cold cake acts as an insulator so the glaze sets fast without melting the filling. Pour quickly and evenly at 110°F — you don't get a second pass once an area sets.
  • Passover: Verify chocolate, agar agar, and vanilla are all certified KFP.
